# MGLabs Fourth & Pixel — Research and Build Notes

Fourth & Pixel is an original MGLabs browser American-football project. It was created after reviewing the broad gameplay loop described on New Star Games' official Retro Bowl pages: retro presentation, simple roster/management systems, player morale/press elements, on-field play calling, career progression, and exhibition-style quick play.

MGLabs intentionally does **not** bundle, mirror, modify, or redistribute Retro Bowl code, art, names, teams, audio, or other game assets. Fourth & Pixel uses original code, fictional presentation, different game logic, and a separate identity.

## Implemented now
- Playable four-down drive loop
- Run, quick pass, deep pass, field goal and punt decisions
- CPU scoring possessions
- Four quarters and final result
- Week/season record
- Local browser persistence
- Team energy and fan-support progression
- Easy / Normal / Hard difficulty
- MGLabs-integrated edition
- Self-contained offline HTML edition at `public/fourth-and-pixel-offline.html`

## Design direction
The goal is the same kind of accessibility that makes retro sports games appealing—quick decisions, readable visuals, short sessions, and progression—without cloning a commercial title.
